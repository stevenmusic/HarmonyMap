# HarmonyMap 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-16

**中文**
HarmonyMap(以下稱「本 App」)**不蒐集、不儲存、不傳送任何個人資料**,沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具。

- 您的設定(語言、主題、樂器、移調夾、慣用手、練習進行等)只存在您裝置上的本機儲存空間,不會上傳,刪除 App 後即清除。
- 真實鋼琴與吉他音色(取樣)已經內建在 App 裡,播放時不需要網路。
- 唯一的網路請求是向 Google Fonts 下載介面字型;對方伺服器只會看到一般連線資訊(例如 IP 位址),本 App 不會附帶任何個人資料。沒有網路時會改用系統字型,功能不受影響。
- 本 App 不使用相機、麥克風、定位、通訊錄或相簿。

如有疑問,請來信:sifan888494@gmail.com

**English**
HarmonyMap ("the App") **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

- Your preferences (language, theme, instrument, capo, handedness, practice progressions, etc.) are kept only in local storage on your device. They are never uploaded and are removed when you delete the App.
- The realistic piano and guitar samples are bundled inside the App; playing sounds needs no network.
- The only network request is downloading interface fonts from Google Fonts; that server sees ordinary connection information (such as an IP address) and the App sends no personal data. Without a connection the App uses system fonts and works the same.
- The App does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library.
